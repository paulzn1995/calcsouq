## The maths
- 4,000 h = 240,000 minutes. If the average viewer watches ~3 min of a 9–10 min video, that is ~80,000 long-video views (~900 a day for 90 days).
- Shorts views do NOT count towards the 4,000 hours. Shorts are for subscribers and for sending people to long videos.
- 1–3 months is a stretch target. It happens only if 2–3 videos break out. So every long video must be a topic many Malayalis are searching for that week, with a hook they cannot ignore.

## Rules for long videos
1. **Malayalam first.** Default every long video to Malayalam narration with English subtitles burnt in (quality standard: build/yt_long_gold_ml.py and build/youtube/long/2026-11-22-gold-fall-malayalam/). Make an English video only for a huge worldwide story.
2. **Length 8–12 minutes** (more watch time per view), with chapters, worked examples in ₹ and a clear "what to do now" scene near the end.
3. **Hook = a real worry, asked as a question,** in the thumbnail and title, e.g. "പണയം വെച്ചവർ കുടുങ്ങിയോ?". The video must answer it honestly with verified numbers. No fake claims, no "guaranteed" or "shocking truth" lines that the video does not back up: YouTube demotes misleading clickbait, and viewers who feel tricked leave early and hurt watch time.
4. **First 30 seconds:** the biggest number on screen in the first 3 s, then say what the viewer will learn ("ഈ വീഡിയോ കഴിയുമ്പോൾ…").
5. **Topic pool** (pick what is trending this week; check Google Trends India/Kerala and YouTube search suggestions):
   - gold: daily price moves, gold loan, pledged-gold auctions, buying for weddings, 916/hallmark, making charges;
   - loans: home-loan EMI after rate changes, personal-loan traps, credit score, prepay or not;
   - savings: FD vs gold vs SIP, KSFE chitty, post office schemes, PF/pension changes;
   - rupee: rupee falling, remittance timing, what a weak rupee does to prices;
   - Kerala: land and property costs, budget announcements, fuel price, tax changes;
   - world events explained for Malayali families (Fed, oil, wars, stock crashes) — always "what it means for your money".
6. **Packaging:** Malayalam + English title (Malayalam first), description with chapters and the CalcSouq tool link, 15–20 mixed Malayalam/English tags, pinned comment with a question, end screen to the previous Malayalam video, add to a Malayalam playlist.
