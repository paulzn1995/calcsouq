## RBI Lending Against Gold and Silver Collateral Directions, 2025 (in force from 1 Apr 2026)
- LTV caps 85% (≤ ₹2.5 lakh), 80% (₹2.5–5 lakh), 75% (> ₹5 lakh), to be maintained through the tenor (RBI notification; Business Standard; Taxguru; Shriram Finance).
- Bullet loans: LTV on the total amount payable at maturity (IIFL Finance blog, quoting the directions); principal and interest repaid within 12 months (Business Standard).
- Valuation: lower of previous day's close or 30-day average; no value for making charges or stones (Business Standard; IIFL).
- Auction: advance notice; reserve price ≥ 90% of market value, 85% after two failed auctions; surplus back within 7 working days (Business Standard; Shriram Finance; Conventus Law).
- Gold returned within 7 working days of closure, ₹5,000/day compensation for delay (Business Standard; IIFL).
- Terms, valuation and notices in the borrower's preferred language (Business Standard).
- EMI loans largely unaffected, bullet loans most exposed (Outlook Money, 30 Jun 2026). Margin call remedies: part-payment, more gold, closure (IIFL).
- Borrowing guidance: stay below 80% of the permitted amount (Business Standard, Jan 2026); 60–65% of assessed value (IIFL).
