## Worked examples (Python)
- ₹5,00,000 for 1 year, quarterly compounding: 6.25% → +₹31,990; 6.75% → +₹34,614.
- ₹5,00,000 for 5 years at 7.20% → ₹7,14,374.
- Prices up 6.09% on ₹5,00,000 → ₹30,450; real gain before tax ₹31,990 − ₹30,450 = ₹1,540; after 10% tax ₹28,791 → ₹1,659 behind prices.
- One month of interest on ₹5,00,000 at 6.25% ≈ ₹2,604; a 0.25% higher rate for one year adds ≈ ₹1,311 (≈ 2.2 weeks of interest).
- ₹10,00,000 at 7.20% for 1 year → ≈ ₹73,967 interest (below the ₹1 lakh senior TDS limit).
- Special tenures (regular depositors): IOB 444 days 6.75%, BoB 555 days 6.85% vs HDFC 5 years 6.60%.
