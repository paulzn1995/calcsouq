## 1. UAE Final Settlement Workbook (Excel)

**Summary line:** Work out your full UAE end-of-service settlement in one spreadsheet: gratuity, leave, salary, notice and deductions.

**Description:**
Leaving a job in the UAE? This workbook puts your whole final settlement on one page, so you can check the figure your employer gives you before you sign.

What's inside:
- Inputs sheet: basic and total salary, dates, leave and notice. Example values are filled in so you can see the format.
- Statement sheet: gratuity (21 days a year for the first 5 years, 30 days after, capped at 2 years' basic), unused leave, unpaid salary days, notice paid or deducted, other amounts and deductions, with the working shown line by line.
- Leave log: record leave as you take it; unpaid days can feed the statement automatically.

Based on UAE Federal Decree-Law 33/2021 (private sector). Works in Excel, Google Sheets and Apple Numbers.
The free online version is at calcsouq.com/uae-final-settlement-calculator.

General information only, not legal advice. Free-zone and government contracts may follow different rules.

## 2. Trading Journal & R-Multiple Tracker (Excel)

**Summary line:** A clean trading journal that measures every trade in R and shows whether your method has an edge.

**Description:**
Most traders track profit and loss. This journal also tracks risk, so you can see what your method is really doing.

What's inside:
- Journal sheet with 500 rows: date, instrument, direction, setup, entry, stop, exit, size, risk and P&L. R-multiple, running balance, peak and drawdown calculate themselves.
- Risk vs plan column that flags trades where you risked more than your planned percentage.
- Stats sheet: win rate, average win and loss in R, expectancy per trade, profit factor, largest drawdown, return, and results by setup.
- Settings sheet for starting balance, planned risk and your own setup names.

Works for forex, CFDs, futures, stocks and crypto. Works in Excel, Google Sheets and Apple Numbers.

For record-keeping only. Not investment advice. Leveraged trading carries a high risk of loss.

**Tags:** trading journal, r multiple, forex, excel template, risk management, expectancy, futures
