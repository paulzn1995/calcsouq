    What is a swap?

    A swap (or rollover) is the interest adjustment applied when you hold a leveraged position past the daily rollover, usually 5 pm New York time. It reflects the interest-rate difference between the two currencies, plus the broker's markup. It can be a charge or a credit.

    Why Wednesday is triple

    Spot forex settles two business days after the trade (T+2). A position held through Wednesday's rollover settles over the weekend, so three days of swap are applied at once. Many index and commodity CFDs apply the triple charge on Friday instead.

    Where to find your swap rates

    In MT4 and MT5, right-click the symbol in Market Watch, choose Specification, and read "Swap long" and "Swap short". If they are quoted in points, multiply by the pip value per point to get money per lot.

    Frequently asked questions

    How is forex swap calculated?

    Swap = lots × swap rate per lot per night × number of nights, with the triple-swap day counted as three nights.

    Why is swap charged three times on Wednesday?

    Because of T+2 settlement, Wednesday's rollover covers the weekend, so three nights are charged together.

    Are there swap-free accounts?

    Many brokers offer Islamic (swap-free) accounts, which may charge a fixed admin fee instead.
