    The progressive NDFL scale

    Since 2025, salary is taxed on a five-step scale. Each rate applies only to the part of the year's income inside its band, counted from January.

    Income for the yearRate on that partTax at the top of the band
    Up to 2.4 million ₽13%312,000 ₽
    2.4–5 million ₽15%702,000 ₽
    5–20 million ₽18%3,402,000 ₽
    20–50 million ₽20%9,402,000 ₽
    Over 50 million ₽22%—

    Example

    Salary 250,000 ₽ a month: 3 million ₽ a year. NDFL is 13% × 2.4 million + 15% × 600,000 = 312,000 + 90,000 = 402,000 ₽. NDFL is 32,500 ₽ a month until September. In October income since January passes 2.4 million ₽, so that month's NDFL is 34,500 ₽, and from November it is 37,500 ₽.

    Different rules apply to some income, such as northern allowances, dividends and interest, and to non-residents. This calculator covers ordinary salary of a tax resident.

    Frequently asked questions

    What is the NDFL rate in 2026?

    13% on salary up to 2.4 million roubles a year, 15% from 2.4 to 5 million, 18% from 5 to 20 million, 20% from 20 to 50 million and 22% above.

    Is the higher rate charged on my whole salary?

    No. Only the part of the year's income above each threshold is taxed at the higher rate.
