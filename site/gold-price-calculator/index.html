    How the price of gold jewellery is worked out

    Final price = (gold rate per gram × weight) + making charges + tax

    • Gold rate: the published rate for the purity you are buying (24K, 22K, 21K or 18K).
    • Making charge: the jeweller's fee for crafting the piece. It is quoted either as a percentage of the gold value or as a fixed amount per gram, and is often negotiable.
    • UAE: 5% VAT is charged on the full price, including making charges. Investment bullion of 99% purity or more is zero-rated.
    • India: 3% GST is charged on the jewellery value, including making charges.

    Converting the 24K rate to other purities

    If you only know the 24K rate, multiply it by the purity: 22K ≈ 91.6%, 21K = 87.5% and 18K = 75%. Tick the box in the calculator to do this automatically. Jewellers' published 22K rates can differ slightly from this conversion.

    Tips before you buy

    • Ask for the gold value, making charge and tax as separate lines on the invoice.
    • Check for hallmarking: BIS HUID in India, or the Dubai Central Laboratory hallmark in Dubai.
    • Stone weight should not be charged at the gold rate.

    Frequently asked questions

    How is the gold jewellery price calculated in Dubai?

    Multiply the gold rate per gram for your purity by the weight, add the making charge, then add 5% VAT on the total.

    Is VAT charged on making charges in the UAE?

    Yes. Retail gold jewellery carries 5% VAT on the full sale value, including making charges.

    What is the GST on gold jewellery in India?

    3% GST is charged on the value of gold jewellery, including making charges.

    What are typical making charges?

    They vary widely by design and shop, from a few percent for plain chains to 20% or more for intricate pieces. Always compare and negotiate.
