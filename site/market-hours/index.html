Key market hours in UAE and India time

  • NSE and BSE India: 09:15–15:30 IST (07:45–14:00 in Dubai).
  • GIFT Nifty: 06:30–15:40 and 16:35–02:45 IST, almost 21 hours a day.
  • DFM and ADX: 10:00–15:00 Gulf Standard Time, Monday to Friday.
  • London: 08:00–16:30 UK time, which is 12:00–20:30 in Dubai in winter.
  • New York: 09:30–16:00 Eastern, which is 18:30–01:00 in Dubai in winter and 17:30–00:00 in summer.

Forex sessions

The forex market runs 24 hours a day, five days a week, passing from Sydney to Tokyo, London and New York. Liquidity and volatility are highest during the London–New York overlap. The times above change when the US, UK and Australia switch daylight saving, and this clock adjusts for that automatically.

Frequently asked questions

What time does the US stock market open in Dubai?

NYSE and Nasdaq open at 9:30 am New York time: 6:30 pm in Dubai in winter, 5:30 pm during US daylight saving time.

What are the NSE market timings in UAE time?

NSE trades 9:15 am to 3:30 pm IST, which is 7:45 am to 2:00 pm in the UAE.

When is the London session in IST?

London opens at 8:00 am UK time: 1:30 pm IST in winter and 12:30 pm IST in British Summer Time.

Is the forex market open on weekends?

No. Forex trading opens on Monday morning in Sydney (Sunday evening in Europe and the US) and closes on Friday at 5 pm New York time.
