    How unused leave is paid in the UAE

    Private-sector employees earn 30 calendar days of paid annual leave for each year of service. When your employment ends, you are entitled to be paid for every unused day, including leave earned in your final, incomplete year, and the payment is calculated on your basic salary (Article 29 of Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021 and Article 19 of Cabinet Resolution No. 1 of 2022).

    Worked example

    Basic salary AED 8,000 → daily wage AED 266.67. You carried 10 unused days, worked 6 months of the final year (earning 15 days) and took 5 days. Unused days = 10 + 15 − 5 = 20. Payment = 266.67 × 20 = AED 5,333.

    Carrying leave forward

    During employment you can usually carry forward up to half of your annual leave to the next year, or agree with your employer to be paid for it. Your employer cannot stop you from taking accrued leave for more than two years unless you choose to carry it over or be paid instead.

    Leave pay vs leave encashment

    When you take annual leave, you receive your normal wage for those days. When unused leave is encashed, the law uses basic salary. Some contracts are more generous, and the better terms apply.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is leave encashment calculated on basic or gross salary in UAE?

    On basic salary, under Article 19 of Cabinet Resolution No. 1 of 2022.

    Do I get paid for unused leave if I resign?

    Yes. Unused leave, including leave earned in your final part year, must be paid when your service ends.

    How many annual leave days do UAE employees get?

    30 calendar days for each completed year of service, earned pro rata in a part year.
