    How the property deduction works

    When you buy or build a home in Russia, you can get back NDFL you have paid. Two separate limits apply per person:

    • Purchase price: up to 2 million ₽ of spending, once in a lifetime, but it can be spread over several homes until used up.
    • Mortgage interest: up to 3 million ₽ of interest, for one home.

    The refund equals the tax you paid on that much income. At 13% that is up to 260,000 ₽ for the price and 390,000 ₽ for interest. With the progressive scale, people whose income is taxed at 15–22% get back more. You can never get back more than the NDFL you paid in a year; the rest carries over to later years.

    Example

    A flat for 8 million ₽ with 1.5 million ₽ of mortgage interest, salary 1.8 million ₽ a year (NDFL 234,000 ₽). The deductions total 3.5 million ₽: 455,000 ₽ back, spread over two years.

    A husband and wife can each claim their own limit. You can claim for the last three years of tax paid. Check the details with the tax service or in your personal account on nalog.gov.ru.

    Frequently asked questions

    How much can I get back for buying a flat in Russia?

    Up to 260,000 roubles for the price (13% of 2 million) and up to 390,000 for mortgage interest (13% of 3 million). More if part of your income is taxed above 13%.

    Can spouses both claim?

    Yes. Each spouse has their own 2 million rouble limit for the price, so a couple can receive up to 520,000 roubles at 13%.
