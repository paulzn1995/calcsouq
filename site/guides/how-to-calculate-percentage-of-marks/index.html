How to calculate percentage of marks

One formula covers every case: add the marks you got, divide by the maximum marks, multiply by 100.

Updated 25 Nov 2026

Use the Percentage Calculator →% of, % change, increase and decrease

The formula

Percentage = (marks obtained ÷ total maximum marks) × 100

Add your marks across all the subjects that count, add the maximum marks for the same subjects, divide the first total by the second and multiply by 100. Round only at the end.

Worked example: 5 subjects out of 100 each

SubjectMarksOut of
English78100
Mathematics91100
Science85100
Social science72100
Second language88100
Total414500

414 ÷ 500 × 100 = 82.8%.

6 subjects, or papers with different maximum marks

With six subjects out of 100, divide by 600. If the papers carry different maximum marks (for example 80 for theory and 20 for practical), do not average the separate percentages. Add the real marks and the real maximums: 65/80 and 18/20 make 83/100, which is 83%, while averaging 81.25% and 90% would give a wrong 85.6%.

Best of five

Some boards and admission forms ask for the percentage of your best five subjects. Drop the subject with the lowest marks, then use a total out of 500. If the example above had a sixth subject with 60 marks, best of five is still 414 out of 500, or 82.8%, while all six give 474 out of 600, or 79%. Always check which subjects your form says must be included; some insist on a language.

Marks you need for a target percentage

Turn the formula round: marks needed = target % × total marks ÷ 100. To reach 75% in a 500-mark exam you need 375 marks. If you already have 290 from four papers, the last 100-mark paper needs 85.

CGPA and grades

A CGPA or grade point is not a percentage. Universities and boards use their own conversion rules, so use the formula printed on your marksheet or in your university's regulations rather than a general multiplier.

The percentage calculator does the division for you: enter the marks obtained as X and the total as Y in the "X is what % of Y" row.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate percentage of marks out of 500?

Divide your total marks by 500 and multiply by 100. For example, 414 ÷ 500 × 100 = 82.8%.

Should I average the percentages of each subject?

Only if every subject has the same maximum marks. Otherwise add the actual marks and the actual maximums first, then divide.

How do I calculate percentage for 6 subjects?

Add the marks of all six subjects, divide by the total maximum (600 if each is out of 100) and multiply by 100.

Can I convert CGPA to percentage with this formula?

No. CGPA conversion depends on the rule set by your board or university; check your marksheet or its regulations.
