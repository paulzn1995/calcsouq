Can an NRI buy land in Kerala?

Yes: NRIs can buy residential and commercial property in Kerala. Agricultural land, plantations and farmhouses are the exception.

What NRIs and OCIs can buy

  • Allowed: residential and commercial property, with no limit on the number of properties.
  • Not allowed by purchase: agricultural land, plantation property and farmhouses. They can be received by inheritance.

How to pay

Pay through normal banking channels: from an NRE, NRO or FCNR account, or by inward remittance from the UAE. Cash and payment in foreign currency are not allowed.

Registering while you are in the Gulf

You can sign at the sub-registrar office on a visit, or give a relative a power of attorney. Execute it at the Indian embassy or consulate (or before a notary and attest it there), then have it adjudicated for stamp duty in Kerala before use.

Checks before buying

  • Encumbrance certificate (at least 13–30 years)
  • Title deeds and prior deeds
  • Land tax receipt and possession certificate
  • Classification in revenue records: wetland (nilam) and paddy land have strict conversion rules

Frequently asked questions

Can NRIs buy agricultural land in Kerala?

No. NRIs and OCIs cannot buy agricultural land, plantations or farmhouses, though they can inherit them.

Can I register property in Kerala while living in the UAE?

Yes, through a power of attorney executed at the Indian embassy or consulate and given to someone in Kerala.
