How many square feet are in 1 cent?

One cent is 435.6 square feet, or one hundredth of an acre.

Other Kerala land units

  • 1 acre = 100 cents = 43,560 sq ft
  • 1 are = 100 m² = 1,076.39 sq ft ≈ 2.47 cents
  • 1 hectare = 100 ares ≈ 247.1 cents ≈ 2.47 acres

Sale deeds and brokers in Kerala usually quote cents, while village and revenue records often use ares. Convert one to the other before you compare prices or check a document.

Price per cent to price per sq ft

Divide the price per cent by 435.6. Land at ₹5 lakh per cent works out to about ₹1,148 per sq ft.
