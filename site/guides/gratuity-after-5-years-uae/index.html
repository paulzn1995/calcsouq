How much gratuity do you get after 5 years in the UAE?

Five years is the point where the UAE gratuity rate jumps from 21 to 30 days of basic salary a year. Here is what that means in dirhams.

Updated 20 Nov 2026

Use the UAE Gratuity Calculator →End-of-service benefit under the new labour law

The short answer

After exactly five years you receive 105 days of basic salary (21 days × 5 years). From year six onwards, every extra year adds 30 days. The total is capped at two years' basic salary.

Why year six matters

Staying a sixth year earns you 30 days' pay for that year instead of 21, which is about 43% more per year. If you are deciding when to leave, finishing year five and then working part of year six both raise your payout.

When you hit the cap

Because the total cannot exceed 24 months of basic salary, the cap of 720 days' basic pay is reached after about 25½ years of service (105 days for the first 5 years plus 30 days for each later year).

Frequently asked questions

How much gratuity is paid after 5 years in UAE?

105 days of basic salary: 21 days for each of the 5 years. With AED 10,000 basic, that is AED 35,000.

How much gratuity after 10 years in UAE?

105 days for the first 5 years plus 150 days for the next 5, a total of 255 days of basic pay. With AED 10,000 basic, that is AED 85,000.

Is gratuity paid for part of a year?

Yes. Once you complete one year, extra months and days are paid pro rata.
