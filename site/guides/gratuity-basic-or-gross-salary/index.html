Is gratuity calculated on basic or gross salary?

Your gratuity is calculated on your last basic salary only. Housing, transport and other allowances don't count.

Why it matters

Many UAE packages put only 50–60% of total pay into basic salary. Two people with the same total pay can therefore get very different gratuity.

What counts as basic

Basic salary is the figure stated as "basic" in your contract and MOHRE offer letter. Commission, overtime and allowances are excluded unless the contract says otherwise.

Tip when negotiating

When you compare job offers, look at the basic salary as well as the total. A higher basic raises your gratuity and often your leave encashment.

Frequently asked questions

Does housing allowance count for gratuity?

No. Gratuity is calculated on the last basic salary only, unless your contract explicitly includes allowances.

Which salary is used, first or last?

Your last basic salary at the end of employment.
