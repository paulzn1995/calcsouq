How much is 1 pip in gold (XAU/USD)?

One standard lot of gold is usually 100 troy ounces, so every $1 move in price is worth $100 per lot.

Why "pip" is confusing for gold

Brokers define a gold pip differently: some use $0.01, many use $0.10, and some traders call $1 a pip. Rather than relying on the word, check the contract size and tick size in MT5 (right-click the symbol, then Specification).

Risk example

With a $1,000 account and 1% risk ($10), a $5 stop on gold allows 10 ÷ (5 × 100) = 0.02 lots.
