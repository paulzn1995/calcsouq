22K vs 24K gold: what's the difference?

Karat measures how much of a piece is pure gold. More karats means purer, softer and more expensive gold.

Which should you buy?

  • For investment: 24K coins or bars. Making charges are low, and in the UAE investment gold of 99% purity or more is zero-rated for VAT.
  • For traditional jewellery: 22K is the Indian standard and holds value well, but it is soft.
  • For everyday wear or stones: 18K is harder and holds settings securely, but less of what you pay for is gold.

Converting rates between karats

Multiply the 24K rate by the purity. If 24K is AED 340 a gram, 22K is about 340 × 0.916 = AED 311. Published shop rates may differ slightly.

What "916" means

916 is the hallmark for 22K: 916 parts pure gold per 1,000. In India, look for the BIS hallmark with a 6-digit HUID code.

Frequently asked questions

Is 22K or 24K better for jewellery?

22K. 24K is too soft for most jewellery and bends and scratches easily.

What does 916 gold mean?

It means 91.6% pure gold, the same as 22 karat.
