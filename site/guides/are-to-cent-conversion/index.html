Are to cent conversion

Revenue records in Kerala often list land in ares and hectares. Sellers quote cents. Here's how to convert.

Key conversions

  • 1 cent = 0.4047 ares
  • 1 hectare = 100 ares = 247.1 cents

Why it matters

If a deed says 10 cents but the record shows 3.5 ares (8.65 cents), the land is smaller than described. Check the survey number and area on the government land records before paying an advance.
