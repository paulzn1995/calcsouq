Gold making charges explained

The making charge is often the biggest negotiable part of a jewellery bill.

Updated 20 Nov 2026

Use the Gold Jewellery Price Calculator →Rate × weight + making charge + VAT or GST

What is a making charge?

It is the jeweller's fee for designing and crafting the piece, on top of the gold value. It is quoted as a percentage of the gold value or as a fixed amount per gram.

Typical ranges

  • Plain chains and bangles: low, often a few percent or a small amount per gram.
  • Machine-made designs: moderate.
  • Handcrafted, antique or temple designs: highest, sometimes 20% or more.

Tax on making charges

UAE: 5% VAT is charged on the full price, gold value plus making charge. Tourists can reclaim it on departure.
India: 3% GST is charged on the jewellery value including making charges.

How to pay less

  • Ask for the making charge as a separate line, then compare two or three shops.
  • Choose simpler designs for investment-type purchases.
  • Watch for festival offers (Akshaya Tritiya, Dhanteras, Dubai Shopping Festival) that discount making charges.
  • Check the exchange or buyback terms, because making charges are usually not refunded when you sell.

Frequently asked questions

Is VAT charged on making charges in Dubai?

Yes. 5% VAT is charged on the full retail price, including making charges.

Are making charges refunded when selling gold?

Usually not. Jewellers generally pay only for the gold content, often minus a deduction.
