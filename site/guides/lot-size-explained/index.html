Forex lot sizes explained

A lot is the trade size. On MT4 and MT5 you type it as a decimal: 1.00, 0.10 or 0.01.

How to choose a lot size

Don't choose a lot size first. Decide how much you are willing to lose (for example 1% of the account) and where your stop loss goes, then work out the lot size:

Lots = risk amount ÷ (stop in pips × pip value per lot)

Example: $5,000 account, 1% risk = $50, 20-pip stop on EUR/USD → 50 ÷ (20 × 10) = 0.25 lots.

Lots on other products

CFDs on indices, oil and gold have their own contract sizes (gold is usually 100 oz per lot). Always check the symbol specification before trading.

Frequently asked questions

What is 0.01 lot?

A micro lot: 1,000 units of the base currency, worth about $0.10 per pip on EUR/USD.

How much money do I need for 1 lot?

That depends on leverage. At 1:100, one lot of EUR/USD needs about 1% of its value, around $1,085 at 1.0850.
