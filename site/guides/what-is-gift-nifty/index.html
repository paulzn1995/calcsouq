What is GIFT Nifty?

GIFT Nifty is a dollar-denominated Nifty 50 futures contract traded at NSE International Exchange in GIFT City, Gujarat.

From SGX Nifty to GIFT Nifty

For years, offshore investors traded Nifty futures on the Singapore Exchange as SGX Nifty. From 3 July 2023 that trading moved to NSE IX in GIFT City and was renamed GIFT Nifty. SGX members can still route orders to it.

How traders use it

Because GIFT Nifty trades before the NSE opens at 09:15 IST, its level compared with the previous Nifty 50 close hints at a gap-up or gap-down open. It reflects overnight US markets, Asian markets, crude oil and news.

It is a futures price, so it includes a small premium over the spot index. The actual opening gap is often a little smaller than GIFT Nifty suggests.

Frequently asked questions

Is SGX Nifty the same as GIFT Nifty?

GIFT Nifty is the successor. SGX Nifty trading moved to NSE IX in GIFT City in July 2023.

What time does GIFT Nifty open in UAE?

05:00 UAE time for the first session and 15:05 for the second.
