How to get the most rupees when sending money from the UAE

Compare providers on how many rupees arrive in India, not on the headline rate.

Try the AED to INR Remittance Calculator →Compare exchange houses by rupees received

Two costs, not one

Every transfer has an exchange-rate margin (the difference from the market rate) and usually a fixed fee. A provider can advertise "zero fee" and still be expensive through a weaker rate.

The effective rate

Effective rate = rupees received ÷ total dirhams paid (amount + fee)

Example: sending AED 2,000.
Provider A: rate 24.10, fee AED 22 → ₹48,200 for AED 2,022 → effective 23.84.
Provider B: rate 24.00, fee 0 → ₹48,000 for AED 2,000 → effective 24.00.
Provider B wins even though its headline rate is lower.

Tips

  • Send fewer, larger transfers so the fixed fee matters less.
  • Compare at the same time of day, because rates change through the day.
  • Use an NRE account if you may want to move the money back out of India.

Frequently asked questions

What is the cheapest way to send money from UAE to India?

Whichever provider gives the highest effective rate for your amount on the day. Compare by rupees received, not the advertised rate.

Do zero-fee transfers cost nothing?

Not necessarily. The cost is often built into a weaker exchange rate.
