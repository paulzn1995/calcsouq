Why is there a triple swap on Wednesday?

Hold a forex position past Wednesday's rollover and you pay (or earn) three days of swap at once.

Updated 22 Nov 2026

Use the Forex Swap Calculator →Overnight rollover with triple-swap days

The reason: T+2 settlement

Spot forex settles two business days after the trade date. A position held over Wednesday's 5 pm New York rollover moves its value date from Friday to Monday, which covers Saturday and Sunday. So three days of interest are applied at once.

Indices and CFDs

Many index, commodity and stock CFDs charge the triple financing on Friday instead, because they don't follow forex settlement. Check your broker's specification.

Example

Swap long on a pair is −$7.50 per lot per night. Holding 2 lots from Monday to the following Monday: 5 rollovers, one of them triple → 7 nights × 2 lots × −7.50 = −$105.

Positive swap

If you hold the higher-interest currency, swap can be a credit, and Wednesday then pays you three times.

Frequently asked questions

What time is the forex rollover?

Usually 5 pm New York time, which is 2 am in the UAE in winter and 1 am during US daylight saving time.

Do swap-free accounts pay triple swap?

Islamic (swap-free) accounts don't charge swap, but some brokers charge a fixed admin fee after a set number of days.
