ES vs MES: E-mini vs Micro E-mini S&P 500

MES is one tenth the size of ES. The price is the same; only the dollar value of each move differs.

Which to trade

  • Micros let you size precisely and keep risk small. 10 MES equal 1 ES.
  • Full-size contracts cost less in commission per dollar of exposure once your account is large.

Example: a 10-point move in the S&P is $500 on one ES and $50 on one MES. Margin requirements are set by CME and your broker and change with volatility.

Frequently asked questions

How many MES equal one ES?

Ten. MES is one tenth the size of ES.

What is the point value of MES?

$5 per index point, or $1.25 per 0.25 tick.
