How much does land registration cost in Kerala?

Budget about 10% of the property value for government charges, plus a little for the document writer and other costs.

Try the Kerala Stamp Duty Calculator →8% stamp duty + 2% registration fee

The two government charges

  • Stamp duty: 8%
  • Registration fee: 2%

Both are calculated on the higher of the government fair value and the sale consideration in the deed. The same rates apply to men, women and joint buyers.

Other costs to budget

  • Document writer's or advocate's fee
  • Encumbrance certificate and copies of records
  • Mutation (pokkuvaravu) at the village office after registration

Gift and settlement deeds between close family have much lower duty. Confirm the current rates at keralaregistration.gov.in or your sub-registrar office.

Frequently asked questions

What is the total registration cost in Kerala?

About 10% of the higher of fair value and sale price: 8% stamp duty plus a 2% registration fee.

Is the fair value or the sale price used?

Whichever is higher.
