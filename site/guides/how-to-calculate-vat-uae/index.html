How to calculate 5% VAT in the UAE

Two formulas cover almost every VAT sum in the UAE.

Try the UAE VAT Calculator →Add or remove 5% VAT in AED

Adding VAT

Total = net price × 1.05
AED 2,400 × 1.05 = AED 2,520, of which VAT is AED 120.

Removing VAT from a total

Net price = total ÷ 1.05
AED 2,520 ÷ 1.05 = AED 2,400. VAT = 2,520 − 2,400 = AED 120.

A quick shortcut: the VAT inside a VAT-inclusive price is total × 5 ÷ 105, or about 4.76% of the total.

The common mistake

Taking 5% off the total (2,520 × 0.95 = 2,394) gives the wrong answer. VAT was added to the net price, so you must divide by 1.05, not subtract 5%.

Frequently asked questions

What is 5% VAT on AED 1,000?

AED 50, making the total AED 1,050.

How do I find the VAT in a VAT-inclusive price?

Multiply the total by 5 and divide by 105. For AED 1,050 that is AED 50.
