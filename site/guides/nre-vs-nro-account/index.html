NRE vs NRO account: which should you use?

Both are Indian bank accounts for NRIs. The difference is where the money comes from and whether it can go back out.

Which to use

  • Send your UAE salary to an NRE account: the interest is tax-free in India and the money can always go back out.
  • Use an NRO account for income that arises in India, such as rent from your Kerala house.

Rules and tax treatment can change, and the UAE has no personal income tax. Check with your bank or a chartered accountant for your situation.

Frequently asked questions

Is NRE interest taxable in India?

No. Interest on NRE deposits is exempt from Indian income tax while you remain an NRI.

Can I deposit rent from India into an NRE account?

No. Indian income such as rent must go into an NRO account.
