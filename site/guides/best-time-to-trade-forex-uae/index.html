Best time to trade forex from the UAE

The best hours in Dubai are roughly 4 pm to 9 pm, when London and New York are both open.

The UAE has no daylight saving; times shift when the UK, US and Australia change clocks. Session boundaries are approximate.

The best window

The London–New York overlap (about 17:00–21:00 in winter, 16:00–20:00 in summer) has the most volume in EUR/USD, GBP/USD and gold, with tight spreads and big moves. US data such as non-farm payrolls arrives at 17:30 UAE time in winter.

Quiet times

Late evening to early morning in the UAE (after the New York close until Tokyo opens) is thin. Spreads widen around the daily rollover.

Frequently asked questions

What time does the London session open in UAE?

12:00 noon UAE time in winter and 11:00 during UK summer time.

What time does the New York session open in Dubai?

Around 17:00 in winter and 16:00 during US daylight saving time.
