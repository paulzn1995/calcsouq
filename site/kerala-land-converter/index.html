    Land in Kerala is usually sold by the cent, while revenue records (the thandaper and the Bhoomi records) often list area in ares and hectares. One are is 100 square metres, and one hectare is 100 ares.

    Quick examples

    • 5 cents = 2,178 sq ft, about the size of a typical house plot.
    • 10 cents = 4,356 sq ft = 404.7 m².
    • 50 cents = half an acre = 21,780 sq ft.

    Frequently asked questions

    How many square feet are in 1 cent?

    One cent is 435.6 square feet, or about 40.47 square metres.

    How many cents are in 1 acre?

    There are 100 cents in one acre.

    How many cents are in 1 are?

    One are is 100 square metres, which is about 2.471 cents.

    How do I convert hectare to cent?

    Multiply hectares by 247.1. For example, 0.5 hectare is about 123.55 cents.
