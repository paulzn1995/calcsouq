About CalcSouq

Last updated 20 November 2026

CalcSouq is a free collection of calculators for people who live, work and invest between the UAE and India: end-of-service gratuity, VAT, gold jewellery prices, remittances, trading risk and Kerala property.

How we build the tools

Each calculator follows the published rule it is based on, such as the UAE Labour Law for gratuity, the FTA for VAT, or exchange contract specifications for futures, and shows the formula and a worked example so you can check the result yourself. Pages are reviewed when rules change.

Who runs the site

CalcSouq is run by a dealing and operations professional with several years' experience across forex, CFDs, futures and equities at a regulated broker in Dubai.

Independence

The site is funded by advertising and is not affiliated with any bank, exchange house, broker or government body.
