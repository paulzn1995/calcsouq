    How to get a photo under 20 KB

    File size depends mostly on the number of pixels and the JPG quality. A phone photo of 12 megapixels cannot fit in 20 KB at full size, but a 300 × 400 pixel version easily can.

    1. Choose your photo. The maximum is already set to 20 KB.
    2. Keep 300 × 400 px, or type the size your form asks for.
    3. The tool tries quality levels until the file is just under 20 KB, so you keep as much detail as possible.
    Pixel sizeTypical JPG size at good quality
    140 × 60 (signature)3–8 KB
    150 × 2008–15 KB
    300 × 40018–35 KB
    600 × 80060–120 KB

    These are typical ranges; busy backgrounds make files larger. If 20 KB still is not reached, lower the pixel size.

    Forms that ask for 10–20 KB

    Signatures for bank and many government exams are commonly 10–20 KB. Choose Signature in the list for 140 × 60 px.

    Before you upload

    • Use a recent photo against a plain light wall, in daylight, with no shadow behind you.
    • If the portal says the file is too small, raise the width and height a little; if it is too large, lower the maximum KB.
    • Your photo is processed in your own browser and is not uploaded anywhere.
    • Rules can change between notifications: check the one you are applying under.

    Frequently asked questions

    How can I reduce a photo to 20 KB?

    Choose the photo, keep the maximum at 20 KB and download. If the result is still too big, lower the width and height.

    Will reducing to 20 KB make my photo blurry?

    Only slightly at small pixel sizes. The tool uses the highest JPG quality that fits, and keeping the pixel size to what the form asks for keeps it sharp.

    Why is my file below the minimum KB?

    Some forms ask for 10–20 KB. Set the minimum to 10 and increase the pixel size a little if the file is too small.
