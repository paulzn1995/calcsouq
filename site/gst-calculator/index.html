    GST rates since 22 September 2025

    Under the GST 2.0 changes recommended by the 56th GST Council meeting, most goods and services now fall into two main slabs, 5% and 18%, with a 40% rate for a narrow list of luxury and "sin" goods and 0% for nil-rated essentials. The old 12% and 28% slabs were largely removed. Some special rates remain, such as 3% on gold and silver.

    CGST, SGST and IGST

    For a sale within one state, GST is split equally between central (CGST) and state (SGST) tax. For a sale between states, the whole amount is IGST.

    Rates depend on the HSN/SAC classification of the item; check the current rate notification for your product or service.

    Frequently asked questions

    What are the GST rates in India in 2026?

    The main slabs are 5% and 18%, with 40% for selected luxury and sin goods and 0% for nil-rated items, effective from 22 September 2025.

    How do I remove GST from a price?

    Divide the GST-inclusive price by 1 plus the rate. At 18%, ₹11,800 ÷ 1.18 = ₹10,000.

    How is GST split into CGST and SGST?

    For sales within a state, half of the GST is CGST and half is SGST. Sales between states carry IGST at the full rate.
