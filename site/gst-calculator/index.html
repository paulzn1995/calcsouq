    Adding and removing tax: worked example at 18%

    TaskFormulaExample
    Add taxamount × 1.1810,000 → 11,800
    Remove taxamount ÷ 1.1811,800 → 10,000
    Tax inside an inclusive priceamount × 18 ÷ 11811,800 → 1,800

    A common mistake is to take 18% off an inclusive price: 11,800 − 18% = 9,676, which is wrong. Always divide by 1 plus the rate.

    India: CGST, SGST and IGST

    Since 22 September 2025 most goods and services in India fall into two main GST slabs, 5% and 18%, with 40% for a short list of luxury and "sin" goods. For a sale within one state, GST is split equally between central (CGST) and state (SGST) tax. For a sale between states, the whole amount is IGST.

    Rates change with budgets and depend on the exact item (HSN/SAC code in India). Check the current rate with your tax authority before you issue an invoice.

    Frequently asked questions

    What are the GST rates in India in 2026?

    The main slabs are 5% and 18%, with 40% for selected luxury and sin goods and 0% for nil-rated items, in force since 22 September 2025.

    How do I remove GST or VAT from a price?

    Divide the tax-inclusive price by 1 plus the rate. At 18%, 11,800 ÷ 1.18 = 10,000. At 20% VAT, 120 ÷ 1.2 = 100.

    How is GST split into CGST and SGST?

    For sales within a state, half of the GST is CGST and half is SGST. Sales between states carry IGST at the full rate.

    Which country does the calculator open on?

    It picks a country from the page language or your device's time zone. Change it in the Country list, or add ?country=GB (or another code) to the address.
