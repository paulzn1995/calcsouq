Why the headline rate isn't the whole story

Exchange houses and apps make money in two ways: the exchange rate margin and a fixed transfer fee. A provider with a slightly lower rate but no fee can beat one with a better rate and a high fee, especially for smaller amounts.

This calculator converts every quote into an effective rate: the rupees your family actually receives divided by everything you pay in dirhams. The highest effective rate is the best deal.

Tips for sending money from the UAE to India

  • Compare quotes on the same day and at the same time, because rates move during the day.
  • Larger transfers dilute the fixed fee. Combining two small transfers into one usually saves money.
  • Remittances to an NRE account can be repatriated. Money sent to an NRO account may face limits on taking it back out.
  • The AED is pegged to the US dollar at 3.6725, so the AED/INR rate moves with USD/INR.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate AED to INR?

Multiply the dirham amount by the rupees-per-dirham rate. For example, AED 1,000 × 24.10 = ₹24,100.

What is an effective exchange rate?

It is the rupees received divided by the total dirhams you pay, including fees. It lets you compare providers fairly.

Is it cheaper to send money through an app or an exchange house?

It depends on the day's rate and fee. Enter both quotes in this calculator to see which delivers more rupees.

Why is the AED to INR rate linked to the US dollar?

The UAE dirham is pegged to the US dollar at 3.6725, so AED/INR moves almost exactly with USD/INR.
