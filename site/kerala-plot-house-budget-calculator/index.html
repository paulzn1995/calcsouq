    What the budget includes

    • Land: plot size in cents × agreed price per cent.
    • Stamp duty and registration: 8% + 2% of the higher of the sale price and the government fair value.
    • Broker: commonly 1–2% of the land price in Kerala, often charged to both buyer and seller.
    • Legal and documents: document writer or advocate, encumbrance certificate, copies and mutation.
    • Construction: built-up area × cost per square foot. Costs vary widely with finish, location and labour, so get quotes from local contractors.
    • Extras: building permit fees, KSEB and water connections, compound wall, gate and well or borewell.

    Tips for NRIs building from the Gulf

    • Check the land classification first. Paddy land and wetland have strict conversion rules.
    • Pay through banking channels from your NRE/NRO account and keep every receipt.
    • Release construction payments in stages against work completed and photographs.

    Frequently asked questions

    What is the total cost of registering land in Kerala?

    About 10% of the higher of fair value and sale price (8% stamp duty + 2% registration), plus document writer and other small fees.

    How many sq ft is 10 cents?

    4,356 square feet.
