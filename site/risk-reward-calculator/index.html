    What risk–reward tells you

    Risk–reward compares how much you could lose (entry to stop loss) with how much you could gain (entry to take profit). A 1:2.5 trade risks 1 to make 2.5. This is often written as a 2.5R target.

    Breakeven win rate

    Breakeven win rate = 1 ÷ (1 + R). At 1:1 you must win over 50% of trades; at 1:2, over 33%; at 1:3, over 25%. Spreads, commission and slippage push the real figure a little higher.

    Frequently asked questions

    What is a good risk–reward ratio?

    Many traders look for at least 1:1.5 or 1:2, but what matters is the ratio together with your real win rate.

    What win rate do I need at 1:2 risk–reward?

    More than 33.3% of trades, before trading costs.
