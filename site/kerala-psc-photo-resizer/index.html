    Kerala PSC photo rules

    For One Time Registration on the Thulasi portal, Kerala PSC asks for a JPG photo 150 pixels wide and 200 pixels high, no larger than 30 KB, with the candidate's name and the date the photo was taken printed at the bottom. The photo must be recent; new registrations need a photo taken within the last six months.

    How to do it here

    1. Tap Choose photo and pick a clear front-facing photo.
    2. Drag and zoom until your head and the top of your shoulders fill the frame above the white band.
    3. Type your name exactly as in your PSC profile and set the date the photo was taken.
    4. Download. The file is 150 × 200 px and typically 8–20 KB.

    Common rejection reasons

    • Name or date missing, or a date that does not match when the photo was taken.
    • Wrong pixel size (for example 200 × 150 instead of 150 × 200).
    • File above 30 KB, or saved as PNG instead of JPG.
    • Dark or patterned background.

    Before you upload

    • Use a recent photo against a plain light wall, in daylight, with no shadow behind you.
    • If the portal says the file is too small, raise the width and height a little; if it is too large, lower the maximum KB.
    • Your photo is processed in your own browser and is not uploaded anywhere.
    • Rules can change between notifications: check the one you are applying under.

    Frequently asked questions

    What is the photo size for Kerala PSC?

    150 pixels wide × 200 pixels high, JPG, up to 30 KB, with name and date of photo at the bottom.

    How do I add name and date to a PSC photo?

    Tick 'Add name and date at the bottom', type your name and the photo date, then download. The band is added inside the 150 × 200 image.

    How long is a PSC photo valid?

    Kerala PSC notifications say a photo uploaded meeting all requirements is valid for 10 years from the date of uploading; new registrations need a photo taken within the last six months.
