    What you pay on top of the price

    Almost every country charges something when a property changes hands, but the amounts differ a lot. The calculator starts from the usual figures for the country you pick and lets you change each one.

    CountryMain government charge (buyer)Other usual costs
    IndiaStamp duty 4.9–8% + registration about 1–2%, set by each state, on the higher of price and government valueBroker about 1–2%, document writer or lawyer
    UAE (Dubai)4% DLD transfer fee + about AED 580 admin + trustee feeAgent 2% + VAT
    UK (England)Stamp Duty Land Tax in bands from 0% to 12%Solicitor, survey
    ChinaDeed tax 1–2% for homesAgent fee
    RussiaState duty of 2,000 roublesNotary (some deals), agent

    India: state by state

    Stamp duty is a state subject, so the rate depends on where the property is. In Kerala it is 8% stamp duty plus 2% registration on the higher of the sale price and the fair value. Several states, such as Delhi, Maharashtra and Uttar Pradesh, charge less when a woman is the buyer. Pick the state and the buyer, then check the figure with your sub-registrar.

    Example

    A ₹50 lakh plot in Kerala with a fair value of ₹40 lakh: stamp duty 8% of ₹50 lakh = ₹4 lakh, registration 2% = ₹1 lakh, broker 1% = ₹50,000 and documents ₹20,000. Total about ₹55.7 lakh, or 11.4% above the price.

    United Kingdom example

    A £350,000 home bought by a home mover in England: 0% on the first £125,000, 2% on the next £125,000 (£2,500) and 5% on the last £100,000 (£5,000). Stamp Duty Land Tax is £7,500.

    Rates and fees change. Treat the result as a planning estimate and confirm the final figures with the registrar, land department or your lawyer.

    Frequently asked questions

    What are stamp duty and registration charges in Kerala?

    8% stamp duty and 2% registration fee on the higher of the sale price and the government fair value.

    Which Indian states charge less stamp duty for women?

    Several, including Delhi, Maharashtra and Uttar Pradesh. Choose 'Woman' as the buyer to apply the usual concession, and confirm with your state department.

    How much does it cost to buy property in Dubai?

    About 4% DLD transfer fee, around AED 580 in admin fees and a trustee fee of AED 2,000–4,000 plus VAT, plus 2% agent fee if you use one.

    Is there stamp duty when buying a flat in Russia?

    No. Buyers pay a state registration duty of 2,000 roubles for an individual, plus any notary and agent fees.
