    Common mistakes

    • A 20% rise followed by a 20% fall does not return to the start: 100 → 120 → 96.
    • To remove a percentage that was added (for example tax included in a price), divide, do not subtract: 1,180 ÷ 1.18 = 1,000.
    • Percentage points are not percentages: a rate moving from 4% to 5% is a 1 percentage point rise but a 25% increase.

    Frequently asked questions

    How do I calculate a percentage of a number?

    Divide the percentage by 100 and multiply by the number. 18% of 2,500 = 0.18 × 2,500 = 450.

    How do I calculate marks percentage?

    Divide marks obtained by total marks and multiply by 100. 432 out of 500 = 86.4%.

    How do I work out percentage increase?

    Subtract the old value from the new value, divide by the old value and multiply by 100.
