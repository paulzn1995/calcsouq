    How to get a photo accepted first time

    Online forms check four things separately: the size in pixels, the file size in KB, the file format and, for many exams, a name and date on the photo. This tool handles all four in one step:

    1. Choose your photo and pick the form from the list, or enter your own width, height and KB limits.
    2. Drag and zoom until your face fills most of the frame, with a little space above the head.
    3. Tick Add name and date if the notification asks for it.
    4. Download. The tool lowers the JPG quality only as far as needed to fit under the maximum KB.

    Worked example: Kerala PSC

    A phone photo of 3024 × 4032 pixels is about 3 MB. Choose Kerala PSC photo, zoom so your face fills the frame, add your name and today's date, and download: the result is 150 × 200 pixels and typically 8–15 KB, well under the 30 KB limit.

    Tips

    • Stand facing a plain light wall in daylight; avoid shadows behind you.
    • For a signature, sign in black ink on white paper, photograph it flat, then choose Signature.
    • If a portal says the file is too small, raise the width and height slightly; some portals count 1 KB as 1,000 bytes rather than 1,024.

    Privacy

    Your photo is processed by your own browser. It is not uploaded to CalcSouq or anyone else.

    Frequently asked questions

    How do I reduce a photo to under 50 KB?

    Choose your photo, set the maximum size to 50 KB and download. The tool lowers the JPG quality step by step until the file fits, keeping the pixel size you chose.

    What is the Kerala PSC photo size?

    150 pixels wide by 200 pixels high, JPG, up to 30 KB, with the candidate's name and the date of the photo printed at the bottom.

    What size is 3.5 × 4.5 cm in pixels?

    About 413 × 531 pixels at 300 DPI. Some portals use 200 DPI (about 276 × 354 pixels); follow the pixel size in your notification if one is given.

    Is my photo uploaded anywhere?

    No. Cropping, resizing and compression happen in your browser on your own device.
