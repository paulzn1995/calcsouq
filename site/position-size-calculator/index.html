Position size calculator

Size every trade so that hitting your stop loss costs exactly the share of your account you planned to risk.

Position size
—

    Lots = (balance × risk %) ÷ (stop loss in pips × pip value per lot)

    Advertisement

    Why position sizing matters

    Most professional traders risk a fixed 1–2% of their account per trade. The stop loss decides where you are wrong, and the position size decides how much being wrong costs you. Sizing every trade this way keeps a losing streak survivable.

    Worked example

    Balance $10,000, risk 1% = $100. Stop loss 25 pips on EUR/USD, where one lot is worth $10 a pip. Lots = 100 ÷ (25 × 10) = 0.40 lots.

    The calculator rounds down to the nearest 0.01 lot, so you never risk more than you planned. Spreads and commission add a little extra cost on top.

    Frequently asked questions

    How do I calculate lot size in forex?

    Divide the amount you are willing to lose by the stop loss in pips multiplied by the pip value per lot.

    What percentage should I risk per trade?

    Many traders use 0.5–2%. Lower risk means smaller drawdowns during losing streaks.

    Does this work for gold on MT5?

    Yes. Choose XAU/USD and enter your stop loss in $0.01 pips. If your broker counts $0.10 as a pip, multiply your stop by 10 first.
