    How exact age is worked out

    Age is counted in whole years first, then whole months, then the remaining days. If the day of the month has not yet been reached, one month is borrowed and the number of days in the previous month is added.

    Example

    Born 15 June 1995, age as on 1 January 2026: from 15 June 1995 to 15 December 2025 is 30 years 6 months, and from 15 December to 1 January is 17 days, so the age is 30 years 6 months 17 days.

    Checking eligibility for a job or exam

    Notifications usually state an age limit as on a fixed date, such as 1 January of the year. Enter that date in Age as on. A candidate born on the cut-off date itself is usually counted as completing that age on that day, but the notification's own wording decides edge cases and any relaxations for reserved categories.

    Frequently asked questions

    How do I calculate my exact age?

    Enter your date of birth. The calculator counts full years, then full months, then remaining days up to today or any date you choose.

    How do I check age as on a specific date?

    Change the 'Age as on' field to the cut-off date in your notification.

    How many days old am I?

    The 'Total days' line shows the number of days from your birth date to the chosen date.
