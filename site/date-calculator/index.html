    Counting days correctly

    By default the start date is counted and the end date is not, so 1 March to 2 March is 1 day. Tick Include the end date when both days count, for example a hotel stay counted in days, a leave application, or a visa that is valid through its last day.

    Working days

    Working days here are Monday to Friday. Public holidays differ by country and employer, so subtract them yourself. In countries with a Friday–Saturday or Saturday–Sunday weekend, check which days your employer counts.

    Examples

    • Notice period: 30 days from 20 November 2026 is 20 December 2026.
    • 90-day visa: entering on 1 January, day 90 is 31 March in a non-leap year (count with the end date included).

    Frequently asked questions

    How many days are between two dates?

    Enter both dates. The result counts the start date but not the end date unless you tick Include the end date.

    How do I add 90 days to a date?

    Use the second section: choose the date and enter 90. Use a minus sign to go back in time.

    Does it count public holidays?

    No. Working days are Monday to Friday; subtract public holidays separately.
