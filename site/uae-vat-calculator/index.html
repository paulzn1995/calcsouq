    How to calculate VAT in the UAE

    The standard VAT rate in the UAE is 5%, administered by the Federal Tax Authority (FTA).

    • To add VAT: multiply the net price by 1.05. AED 1,000 + 5% = AED 1,050.
    • To remove VAT: divide the VAT-inclusive price by 1.05. AED 1,050 ÷ 1.05 = AED 1,000, so the VAT is AED 50.
    • A common mistake is to take 5% off the gross price. That gives AED 997.50, which is wrong.

    Zero-rated and exempt items

    Some supplies are zero-rated, including exports, international transport, certain education and healthcare services, and investment-grade precious metals. Residential rent and local passenger transport are exempt. Retail gold jewellery is charged at 5%.

    Frequently asked questions

    How do I calculate 5% VAT on a price?

    Multiply the price by 0.05 to get the VAT, or by 1.05 to get the total including VAT.

    How do I remove VAT from a total in the UAE?

    Divide the total by 1.05. The result is the price before VAT, and the difference is the VAT amount.

    Is VAT charged on rent in Dubai?

    Residential rent is exempt from VAT. Commercial rent is charged at 5%.

    Can tourists get a VAT refund in the UAE?

    Yes. Tourists can claim back VAT on eligible purchases from shops in the Planet tax-free scheme when they leave the UAE, minus a processing fee.
