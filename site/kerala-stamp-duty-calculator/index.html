    Stamp duty and registration charges in Kerala

    For an ordinary sale deed in Kerala, the Registration Department charges 8% stamp duty and a 2% registration fee, about 10% in total. Both are worked out on the higher of the government fair value and the sale price in the deed. The rate is the same for men, women and joint buyers, and in panchayat, municipality and corporation areas.

    Worked example

    Fair value ₹25 lakh, sale price ₹30 lakh. Charges are worked out on ₹30 lakh: stamp duty ₹2,40,000 + registration fee ₹60,000 = ₹3,00,000.

    Other deeds

    Gift, settlement and partition deeds between family members, and lease deeds, have different and often much lower rates. Document writer fees and e-stamp charges are extra. Confirm the current rates with the sub-registrar office or keralaregistration.gov.in before registering.

    Buying land in Kerala from the Gulf

    NRIs can buy residential and commercial land in India, but not agricultural land, plantations or farmhouses. If you cannot travel, a registered power of attorney attested at the Indian embassy or consulate lets a relative sign on your behalf.

    Frequently asked questions

    What is the stamp duty in Kerala?

    Stamp duty on a sale deed is 8% of the higher of fair value and sale price, plus a 2% registration fee.

    Is there a lower stamp duty for women in Kerala?

    No. Kerala charges the same 8% stamp duty and 2% registration fee for all buyers.

    What is fair value in Kerala?

    Fair value is the minimum land value notified by the government for each survey number. Charges are based on whichever is higher: the fair value or the deed value.

    Can an NRI buy land in Kerala?

    Yes, NRIs can buy residential and commercial property, but not agricultural land, plantations or farmhouses.
