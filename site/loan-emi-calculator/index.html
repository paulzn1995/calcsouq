    How EMI is calculated

    An EMI (equated monthly instalment) repays a loan in equal monthly amounts. Early instalments are mostly interest; later ones mostly repay the principal. This calculator uses the standard reducing-balance formula used by banks in the UAE and India.

    Flat rate vs reducing rate

    Some UAE car and personal loans are advertised at a flat rate, where interest is charged on the original amount for the whole term. A flat rate costs much more than the same number as a reducing rate, often close to double. Ask the bank for the reducing-balance rate (or APR) and enter that here.

    Ways to pay less interest

    • Choose a shorter tenure if the EMI is affordable.
    • Make part-prepayments, checking any early settlement fee first.
    • Compare offers by total interest, not just the monthly figure.

    Frequently asked questions

    What is the EMI on a 100,000 loan at 6.5% for 4 years?

    About 2,371 per month, with total interest of around 13,800.

    What is the difference between flat and reducing interest?

    A flat rate charges interest on the full original loan for the whole term; a reducing rate charges interest only on the balance still owed, so it costs less for the same headline rate.
