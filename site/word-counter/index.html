    What is counted

    • Words: runs of letters or numbers, including words with apostrophes and hyphens (for example don't and well-known count as one word each).
    • Characters: every letter, space and symbol. Many forms and social platforms limit characters, not words.
    • Reading time: about 230 words a minute; speaking time: about 130 words a minute.

    Frequently asked questions

    How are words counted?

    Each run of letters or numbers counts as one word; hyphenated words and contractions count as one.

    Does it count spaces as characters?

    The Characters line includes spaces; the next line shows the count without spaces.

    Is my text saved?

    Only in this browser tab, so it survives a refresh. Closing the tab or tapping Clear removes it.
