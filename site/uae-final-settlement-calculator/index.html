    What goes into a UAE final settlement

    When your employment ends, your employer must pay all your dues within 14 days. Most settlements are made of five parts, and this calculator puts them on one statement you can print or share:

    1. End-of-service gratuity: 21 days' basic salary per year for the first five years, 30 days per year after that, capped at two years' basic salary. You need at least one year of service.
    2. Unused annual leave: paid on basic salary, including leave earned in your final part year.
    3. Salary for days worked in the final month that haven't been paid yet.
    4. Notice period: if the notice period is cut short, whoever ended it early normally pays the other side the total salary for the unserved days.
    5. Other dues and deductions: bonuses, air tickets or expenses your contract promises, minus loans or advances you owe.

    Worked example

    Basic AED 8,000, total AED 13,000, 5 years 9 months of service, 18 unused leave days and 20 unpaid salary days: gratuity ≈ AED 33,830, leave AED 4,800 and salary AED 8,667, a total of about AED 47,297.

    Before you sign

    • Check the basic salary used matches your latest contract or MOHRE offer letter.
    • Ask for an itemised settlement sheet and compare it with this one.
    • If dues aren't paid, you can complain to MOHRE through its app, website or call centre.

    Frequently asked questions

    How long does my employer have to pay my final settlement?

    All end-of-service dues must be paid within 14 days of the end of the contract.

    Is the final settlement calculated on basic or total salary?

    Gratuity and unused leave use basic salary. Unpaid salary days and notice pay use your total salary.

    Do I lose gratuity if I resign?

    No. Under the new UAE labour law, gratuity is the same whether you resign or are terminated.
