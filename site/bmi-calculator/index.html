BMI calculator

Body mass index for adults, with the WHO category and the healthy-weight range for your height.

Sources
Your BMI
—

    BMI = weight (kg) ÷ height (m)². For adults aged 18 and over. Not a diagnosis.

    BMI categories for adults (WHO)

    BMICategory
    Below 18.5Underweight
    18.5 – 24.9Healthy weight
    25.0 – 29.9Overweight
    30 and aboveObesity

    Example

    Height 170 cm and weight 68 kg: 68 ÷ (1.70 × 1.70) = 23.5, in the healthy range. For 170 cm, that range is about 53.5 kg to 72.0 kg.

    Limits of BMI

    • BMI does not separate muscle from fat, so very muscular people can show a high BMI.
    • For people of South Asian and some other ethnic backgrounds, health risks can start at lower BMI values; some health services use lower cut-offs.
    • It is not designed for children, teenagers or pregnancy. Speak to a doctor about your own health.

    Frequently asked questions

    How is BMI calculated?

    Weight in kilograms divided by height in metres squared. 68 kg at 1.70 m gives 23.5.

    What is a healthy BMI?

    For adults, the WHO classes 18.5 to 24.9 as a healthy weight range.

    Is BMI accurate for everyone?

    No. It is a screening number that does not measure body fat directly; muscle, age and ethnicity affect what it means.
