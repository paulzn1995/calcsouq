    How margin works for each trade type

    • Futures: initial margin is a share of the contract value (price × lot size × lots), or a fixed amount per lot. Exchanges set it from their risk models and change it often, sometimes several times a day.
    • Calendar spreads: buying one month and selling another carries much less risk than two outright positions, so exchanges charge a reduced spread margin.
    • Option buyers pay the premium in full. Option sellers post margin. Defined-risk strategies (credit spreads, iron condors) usually need about the maximum loss; naked short options and short straddles or strangles need the most.

    US option margin rules (Cboe / FINRA minimum)

    A naked short equity option needs the premium plus 20% of the underlying value, less any out-of-the-money amount, with a floor of the premium plus 10% (of the share price for calls, of the strike for puts). Broad-based index options use 15% instead of 20%. A credit spread needs the difference between the strikes; an iron condor needs the wider of its two spreads, because only one side can lose at expiry.

    India, LME and CME

    These exchanges use SPAN-type risk models. Margin for a short option or futures position is the SPAN requirement plus any exposure or additional margin, and hedged positions get a benefit. The calculator gives an estimate from the percentage you enter; for the exact figure, use the exchange's file or your broker's calculator, linked under Sources.

    Example

    Nifty iron condor, 1 lot of 65: sell 25,800 put at 90, buy 25,600 put at 45, sell 26,200 call at 85, buy 26,400 call at 40. Net credit 90 points (₹5,850). Maximum loss is 200 − 90 = 110 points (₹7,150), which is roughly what a SPAN-based broker will block for this hedged position.

    Frequently asked questions

    What is the Nifty lot size now?

    65 for Nifty 50 contracts from the January 2026 expiry. Bank Nifty is 30, FinNifty 60 and Midcap Select 120.

    How much margin is needed for an iron condor?

    Under US rules, the width of the wider spread. On NSE and other SPAN exchanges, usually close to the maximum loss, because the bought wings cap the risk.

    Why is calendar spread margin lower?

    The two legs move together most of the time, so the exchange's risk model gives a large offset. The benefit can shrink near expiry of the near month.

    What is the LME lot size for copper and nickel?

    Copper, aluminium, zinc and lead are 25 tonnes per lot. Nickel is 6 tonnes and tin is 5 tonnes.
