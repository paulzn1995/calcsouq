    How UAE gratuity is calculated

    Under Article 51 of the UAE Labour Law (Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021), a full-time employee in the private sector who completes at least one year of continuous service is entitled to an end-of-service gratuity based on their last basic wage:

    • 21 days' basic salary for each of the first five years of service.
    • 30 days' basic salary for each year after the fifth.
    • Part years are paid pro rata once you pass one year.
    • The total cannot exceed two years' basic salary.

    The daily wage is your monthly basic salary divided by 30. Days of unpaid leave are taken out of your service period.

    Worked example

    Basic salary AED 8,000, service of 3 years and 6 months. Daily wage = 8,000 ÷ 30 = AED 266.67. Gratuity = 266.67 × 21 × 3.5 = AED 19,600.

    Resignation vs termination

    Under the new law, resigning no longer reduces gratuity. You receive the same amount whether you resign or are terminated, and gratuity is still payable even after a dismissal without notice under Article 44 (under the old law it could be forfeited). Your employer may deduct amounts you genuinely owe them.

    Who this calculator does not cover

    UAE nationals in GPSSA pension schemes, government employees, DIFC and ADGM employees (who have their own schemes, such as DEWS) and domestic workers follow different rules. Check with your HR team or MOHRE for your exact entitlement.

    Frequently asked questions

    How much gratuity will I get after 5 years in the UAE?

    After exactly five years you get 21 days' basic salary for each year, which is 105 days' basic pay. With a basic salary of AED 10,000 that is 10,000 ÷ 30 × 105 = AED 35,000.

    Is gratuity calculated on basic or gross salary?

    On your last basic salary only. Housing, transport and other allowances are not included unless your contract says otherwise.

    Do I get gratuity if I resign before one year?

    No. Gratuity is only due after at least one year of continuous service.

    Is gratuity reduced if I resign under the new labour law?

    No. The old reductions for resigning (one third or two thirds) were removed for private-sector employees on the new limited contracts.

    Is there a maximum gratuity amount?

    Yes. The total end-of-service gratuity cannot exceed two years' wages.

    When must my employer pay my gratuity?

    Your employer must pay all end-of-service dues within 14 days of the end of your contract.
